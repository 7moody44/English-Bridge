/*   Course Structure
    Total Content: 10 Levels (Pre-A1 to C1).

    Per Level: 8 Lessons + 1 Final Exam.

    Per Lesson: 5 exercises (mix of multiple-choice, listening, and reading).

    Final Exam: 20 questions recapping the entire level.

    Level Breakdown (CEFR)
    Level 1: Pre-A1 (Absolute Beginner)

    Level 2: A1

    Level 3: A1 / A2

    Level 4: A2

    Level 5: A2 / B1

    Level 6: B1

    Level 7: B1 / B2

    Level 8: B2

    Level 9: B2 / C1

    Level 10: C1

    Question Guidelines
    Answers: Clearly mark the correct option with a checkmark (✅).
    Difficulty: Keep questions strictly matched to the targeted CEFR level (e.g., Level 1 must be strictly Pre-A1).
*/
